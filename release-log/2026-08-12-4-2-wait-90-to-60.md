# Release 4.2 (12 Aug 2026): ping wait cut from 90 to 60 seconds

Run on 1 Nov 2026 with the release-impact skill, from the Rook database (29 Jun to 6 Sep 2026). Every figure was recomputed from the database.

## What changed, and what else shipped the same day
- **Ping wait cut from 90 to 60 seconds** (config.py).
- Ranking weights: closeness 0.45 to 0.60, recent yes-rate 0.40 to 0.25.
- Console filters now persist between sessions.
- Three defect fixes: duplicate notification, capability tag order, report time zone.

Because the weights shipped on the same day, the data cannot separate the effect of the wait from the effect of the weights.

Equal-length check (26 days each side): missed offers 2.3% before and 18.0% after; yes 76.2% and 64.0%. Same result as the full windows.

## Setting check: did everyone switch at once?
| Time until the next hero is offered the job | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| After a missed offer | about 92 seconds (91 to 93), 22 cases, 13 heroes | about 62 seconds (61 to 63), 87 cases, 16 heroes |
| After a refusal | about 22 seconds | about 23 seconds (6 to 40 both times) |

Yes. After 12 Aug there are no gaps near 90 seconds, and before it none near 60. Every hero switched on the same day, so there was no test or staged rollout. The first miss under the new wait was at 09:04 on 12 Aug.

## Step or slide?
Missed offers were 0 to 5% a day through 11 Aug, then 28% on 12 Aug and 48% on 13 Aug, then 27%, 25%, 18%. They eased to 4% and 8% on 17 and 18 Aug, but came back to 13 to 28% a day. It is a step on release day, with partial easing.

## Who moved most
- Four heroes (Farlight, The Undertow, Vesper, Meteor Mite) went from 11 to 14 offers a week to 3 to 5. Their yes rate went from 69 to 81% down to 18 to 29%, and they missed 53 to 64% of offers after.
- Everyone else: yes rate down 3 to 21 points, missed 10 to 18%. Ten of the other twelve heroes got more offers a week (for example Nightwell 15.1 to 18.9, The Gale 13.0 to 18.6), which fits them covering for the four.

## Other explanations tested
- **Time of day:** ruled out. Missed offers rose in every band (00 to 05: 4.8% to 12.9%; 06 to 11: 1.6% to 18.4%; 12 to 17: 1.8% to 17.5%; 18 to 23: 2.5% to 19.0%).
- **A few heroes hiding in the average:** partly. The four heroes explain much of the drop in offers, but the other twelve also rose from 2.1% to 13.9% missed.
- **Geography:** not rerun here. See the area-by-area section of CLAUDE.md, where three areas stand out.
- **Seasonality:** cannot be ruled out. There is no earlier year in the data.
- **Other changes the same day:** the new ranking weights. Cannot be separated.

## Tickets
Agree. Tickets ran 5 to 8 a week before and 20, 27, 32 and 25 in the weeks after. "Gone before I could answer" tickets began the week of 10 Aug (7); "phone quiet" tickets began the week of 17 Aug and peaked in the week of 24 Aug. The two themes overlap by two tickets.

## What can't be said
- The database has outcomes, not how long a hero took to answer, so it cannot show how many would have answered between 60 and 90 seconds.
- Availability, travel times and real points are not in the database.
- A before and after shows what changed, not why.

## Confidence (0 to 95)
- The wait changed for every hero on release day: 92.
- Missed offers jumped on release day: 90.
- The shorter wait alone caused the jump: 45 (the weights shipped the same day).
- The four heroes' drop in offers is a knock-on of the jump: 55 (a reconstruction of their points supports it, but real points aren't stored).

What would firm it up: time-to-accept data, a staged rollout next time, and the real points.
