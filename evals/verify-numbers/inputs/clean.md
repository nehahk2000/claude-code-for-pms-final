# Release 4.2 summary (checked draft)

1. Offers nobody answered rose from 2.3% before 4.2 to 18.0% after (25 of 1,085, then 110 of 611).
2. Jobs nobody took rose from 5.5% to 11.1% (48 of 879, then 49 of 440).
3. The database holds 1,696 offers across 1,319 jobs and 16 heroes.
4. The four hardest-hit heroes (Farlight, The Undertow, Vesper, Meteor Mite) missed 2.9% of offers before 4.2 and 59% after.
5. After 4.2, 82% of the jobs nobody took had only one offer sent.
