THE RULE THAT DID IT

A missed offer costs more than a yes earns, and points never come back

YOUR PROMPT · filed in 01-origin-story/prompts.md, #40can we get more details for Farlight from offer.py, availability.py , routing.py and history.py

A hero's points stay level only if they say yes 60% of the time.

The shorter wait made missed offers jump. Missed offers cost the same 0.12 as a refusal, so points fell. Heroes with low points were offered less, so they never got the chance to climb back. EstimateReal points are not stored, so this is replayed from the real offers.

Replay of the four heroes
All four end at exactly 0.00 points. The other twelve end between 0.72 and 1.00.
No test was run
All 87 missed offers after 12 Aug moved on after about 62 seconds, none after 90. Every hero switched on the same day.
