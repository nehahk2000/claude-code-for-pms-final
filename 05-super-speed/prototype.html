STEP 1

Pick a hero and a rule

Each hero's real offers are replayed in order. Today's rules: a yes adds 0.08, a no or a missed offer takes off 0.12, points never come back. The dashed line uses the rule you pick. This shows what would have happened to the points. It is not a forecast.

STEP 2

Pick a moment

Drag the date, or jump to when the hero is first flagged quiet. A hero is quiet when they have had no offer in their own area for 3 days, or their last 3 offers were all missed.
